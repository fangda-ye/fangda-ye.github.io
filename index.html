About

I am an AI Engineer at TikTok in Singapore, working on the ranking model for e-commerce short videos in TikTok's main feed: scaling it up, speeding up its training, and building agents that automate the modeling workflow. I received my Master of Computing in Computer Science from the National University of Singapore in 2026, and my Bachelor of Engineering from Central South University.

My research so far is on deep research agents: agents that search across text and images and write grounded long-form reports (Deep-Reporter, Doc-Researcher), and ways to evaluate what these agents do along the way, not just what they output (MiroEval). I have also post-trained LLM agents with SFT and RL, for parallel web search and for deciding when to step into a conversation, and worked on physically consistent video generation and test-time training for robot manipulation. I have been fortunate to work with Prof. Shuicheng Yan, Dr. Lidong Bing, and Dr. Kuicai Dong.

I am broadly interested in how agents learn to reason and act: reinforcement learning for LLM agents, test-time verification, self-improving agents, and latent reasoning.

  • MiroEval: Benchmarking Multimodal Deep Research Agents in Process and Outcome
    Fangda Ye*, Yuxin Hu*, Pengxiang Zhu*, Yibo Li*, Ziqi Jin, …, Bryan Hooi, Bo An, Shuicheng Yan, Lidong Bing
    A benchmark of 100 real-world tasks (70 text, 30 multimodal) that scores deep research agents on synthesis quality, factual verification, and the research process itself. Process quality turns out to predict the final outcome.

  • Deep-Reporter: Deep Research for Grounded Multimodal Long-Form Generation
    Fangda Ye*, Kuicai Dong*, Zhifei Xie*, Yuxin Hu*, Yihang Yin, Shurui Huang, Shikai Dong, Chen Zhang, Jianzhu Bao, Shuicheng Yan
    An agent that writes long reports with images, grounded in what it retrieves: multimodal search and filtering, checklist-guided synthesis, and recurrent context management. Comes with M2LongBench, 247 research tasks across 9 domains.

    Mingyang Bao, Fangda Ye, Xihao Chen, Bobo Li, Xiangtai Li, Shengqiong Wu

    Kuicai Dong*, Shurui Huang*, Fangda Ye*, Wei Han, Zhi Zhang, Dexun Li, Wenjun Li, Qu Yang, Gang Wang, Yichao Wang, Chen Zhang, Yong Liu
